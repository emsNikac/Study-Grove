IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
================================================================================

- Use Max purchases: 1 for all cosmetics and upgrades unless intentionally stackable.
- Seeds should usually be repeatable purchases.
- Paths, fences, and decorations can be repeatable purchases unless they are unique structures.
- Visual keys are Kotlin-friendly and asset-friendly names.
- IDs are unique and suitable for local persistence.
- Prices and unlock levels are balanced to create a long progression curve.
